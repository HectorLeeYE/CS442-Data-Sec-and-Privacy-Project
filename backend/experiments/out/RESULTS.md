## E2 — Utility (train on the GREEN release, test on real calls)

Word-bigram LM trained on 400 released ASR-style calls, evaluated on 100 held-out *unredacted* calls. Lower perplexity = the release teaches the model more about real calls. PII-token columns score only the words inside sensitive spans, where the variants differ. Digit shape = share of number-type PII still a number of the same length.

| Training corpus | Test perplexity | PII-token perplexity | PII-token OOV | Digit shape preserved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| raw (upper bound, not releasable) | 13.44 | 60.26 | 7.8% | 100.0% |
| suppress all | 74.94 | 60181.06 | 93.0% | 0.0% |
| pseudonym all | 74.95 | 60203.84 | 93.0% | 0.0% |
| surrogate all | 19.72 | 258.61 | 23.4% | 100.0% |
| GREEN policy | 23.3 | 431.38 | 27.3% | 81.9% |
| GREEN policy, real detector | 22.56 | 353.52 | 19.1% | 81.9% |
